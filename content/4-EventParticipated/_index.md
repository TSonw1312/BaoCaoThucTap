### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)  
&emsp;**Event Name:** GenAI-powered App-DB Modernization workshop  

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00, June 27, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Brief Description of Content and Key Activities:** "An intensive workshop focusing on application and database optimization and modernization, powered by Microservices architecture and Generative AI. Key topics included Domain-Driven Design (DDD) methodologies, Event-Driven Architecture, the evolution of compute infrastructure (exploring the Shared Responsibility Model from EC2 to Serverless Lambda), and the application of Amazon Q Developer as an AI assistant to automate the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)."

&emsp;**Outcomes and Value Gained:** "Acquired practical system design thinking centered around a 'business-first' approach and Ubiquitous Language. Mastered Event Storming techniques to model complex workflows, while understanding how to evaluate technical trade-offs (Synchronous vs. Asynchronous, Functions vs. Containers). Additionally, learned how to leverage Amazon Q Developer to boost coding productivity and gained greater confidence when working with legacy systems."
