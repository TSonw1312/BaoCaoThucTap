> This section presents my personal reflections and feedback based on my internship experience at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd. The comments below summarize my observations and suggestions for improving the internship program.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment was professional, friendly, and highly collaborative. Team members were always willing to provide support whenever I encountered technical challenges or needed clarification on project requirements. The workplace encouraged continuous learning, knowledge sharing, and open communication, allowing me to adapt quickly and work efficiently.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Leader**  
My mentor provided detailed technical guidance throughout the internship while encouraging me to research and solve problems independently before offering assistance. Regular discussions and code reviews helped me improve both my technical knowledge and problem-solving skills. The team leader also ensured that I clearly understood each task and provided constructive feedback whenever necessary.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship tasks were closely related to my Software Engineering major. I had the opportunity to apply knowledge of backend development, system design, cloud computing, and software architecture while also learning modern AWS services and serverless application development, which were not extensively covered in my academic curriculum.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship significantly improved both my technical and soft skills. I gained hands-on experience with AWS services such as Lambda, API Gateway, Cognito, Amazon S3, and Amazon SQS while strengthening my understanding of serverless architecture. In addition, I developed better teamwork, technical documentation, communication, and time management skills through daily collaboration with the development team.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company promotes a positive and supportive working culture where employees respect one another and actively share knowledge. Team members collaborated effectively to complete project milestones and were always willing to assist interns whenever difficulties arose. This environment motivated me to learn continuously and contribute more confidently to the project.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The internship program provided a flexible learning environment together with valuable opportunities to participate in technical discussions, internal knowledge-sharing sessions, and practical project development. The internship experience itself was highly valuable because it allowed me to gain real-world experience with enterprise-level cloud technologies and software development processes.

---

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

The most satisfying part of my internship was having the opportunity to participate in the migration of the Examora system from a traditional Express-based architecture to a Hybrid Serverless architecture on AWS. Being trusted with real development tasks allowed me to gain practical experience and better understand how modern cloud systems are designed and deployed.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**

I believe the internship program could be further improved by providing a more structured onboarding guide and additional technical workshops during the first few weeks. This would help new interns become familiar with the project architecture and development workflow more quickly.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**

Yes. I would definitely recommend this internship because it offers practical experience with modern cloud technologies, provides supportive mentors, and creates an excellent environment for both technical and personal development.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**

I suggest organizing more technical sharing sessions between different teams, allowing interns to learn about various AWS services and software engineering practices beyond their assigned projects.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**

Yes. If given the opportunity, I would be very interested in continuing to work with the team and contributing to future cloud-based projects while further developing my technical expertise.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**

I sincerely appreciate the opportunity to intern at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd. This internship has not only strengthened my technical knowledge but also improved my professional working attitude, communication skills, and confidence in working within a software development team. I believe this experience will be a valuable foundation for my future career.
